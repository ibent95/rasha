# RASHA Brand Identity

> **RASHA — Ruang Impian dalam Satu Genggaman.**

## 1. Brand Essence

RASHA is a modern digital ecosystem designed to bring useful services, products, and experiences into one connected space.

The brand idea combines:

- **Manoratha** — dreams, aspirations, and desired outcomes.
- **Akasha** — open space, universe, possibility, and limitless expansion.

The result is the idea of a **"Ruang Impian" — a universe where ideas can become useful digital solutions.**

### Brand promise

> **We don't just build software. We build a universe of solutions that empowers dreams.**

### Brand personality

- Bold
- Modern
- Human
- Technical
- Ambitious
- Connected
- Growth-oriented

---

## 2. Visual Concept

The master symbol combines an **R**, a **planetary/space motif**, and a **green ecosystem geometry**.

### R — Core

The `R` represents:

- RASHA
- recognition
- the core of the ecosystem
- action and creation

### Green orbital path — Ecosystem

The green orbit represents:

- connection
- growth
- movement
- sustainability
- the ecosystem surrounding the core

The orbit intentionally travels through/around the green geometric form beneath the R. The geometry should remain complete and proportional; it must not appear visually cut off.

### Planet / node — Products and services

The orbital node represents individual products, services, or capabilities that can exist inside the RASHA ecosystem.

### Star — Aspiration

The small star represents:

- dreams
- goals
- direction
- aspiration
- a future worth reaching

### Space

The surrounding dark space represents the room in which the ecosystem can expand. It communicates possibility rather than a literal astronomical story.

## 3. Color Palette — Ember / Forest

The original RASHA palette was intentionally refined so the brand feels more mature and distinctive while retaining its red, orange, green, and black DNA.

| Role | Name | HEX | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foundation | Obsidian | `#10100F` | Depth, technology, premium foundation |
| Primary | Ember Red | `#C83E36` | Confidence, action, identity |
| Secondary | Burnt Orange | `#D66A2C` | Creativity, energy, warmth |
| Accent | Forest | `#3F6B4F` | Growth, ecosystem, balance |
| Light | Warm Ivory | `#F1EDE3` | Clarity, humanity, readability |
| Neutral | Stone | `#918C82` | Secondary text, borders, metadata |

### Color hierarchy

The palette should not be used as six equally strong colors.

Recommended visual weight:

- **60%** Obsidian / neutral backgrounds
- **20%** Ember Red + Burnt Orange
- **10%** Forest
- **5%** Warm Ivory accents / highlights
- **5%** Stone and supporting neutrals

Red and orange establish the identity. Green supports the ecosystem story rather than competing with the primary mark.

### Important rule

Avoid recreating the original bright red → orange → yellow "super-app" gradient.

The refined identity should primarily use **distinct, controlled colors**. Gradients may be used selectively in digital experiences, but the logo must remain recognizable in flat color.

## 4. Logo Direction

### Master logo

The selected direction is **01C.2 — Planetary / refined**, using the preferred **B variant** for the orbital treatment.

Core construction:

1. Red/orange `R`
2. Complete green geometric form beneath the R
3. Green orbital path passing through/behind the green geometry and extending outward
4. Orange planetary node
5. Small ivory star
6. Optional RASHA wordmark

### Design principles

- Keep the R recognizable at small sizes.
- Keep the green geometry complete and proportional.
- The green orbit should visibly continue through the green geometry below the R rather than terminating prematurely.
- Avoid excessive decorative elements.
- Preserve enough negative space around the master mark.
- The symbol must work independently from the wordmark.

## 6. Brand Architecture

RASHA should be treated as the **ecosystem/master consumer brand**, while individual products can live underneath it.

```text
RASHA
│
├── Portal
├── Dynamic Form
├── ERP
├── CRM
├── Notification
├── Analytics
└── Future Products
```

The master RASHA mark remains consistent. Product-specific visual treatments should inherit the same color, geometry, typography, and orbital language.

### Personal relationship

**Ibnu Rohan** is the builder/founder identity behind the ecosystem.

The personal identity may appear alongside RASHA where appropriate, but it should not force the entire RASHA brand to become an "IR" personal monogram.

## 7. Typography

Recommended direction:

### Primary

**Sora**

Use for:

- wordmark-inspired display treatments
- headings
- hero statements
- major brand communications

Characteristics:

- geometric
- modern
- technical
- friendly

### Supporting

**Inter**

Use for:

- UI
- body text
- documentation
- tables
- product interfaces

Typography should remain clean and restrained so the logo and orbital graphics remain the visual focus.

## 8. Tone of Voice

RASHA should communicate with confidence without sounding corporate or exaggerated.

### Prefer

> Build better digital experiences.

> One ecosystem. Unlimited possibilities.

> Technology that moves ideas forward.

> Connect. Build. Empower.

### Avoid

- excessive startup jargon
- empty claims
- overly futuristic language
- unnecessary technical complexity in consumer-facing copy

The brand should feel **capable, optimistic, and useful**.

---

## 9. Visual Language

The visual system should use:

- dark spacious backgrounds
- geometric forms
- controlled orbital curves
- small nodes
- restrained star/point motifs
- red/orange focal elements
- green ecosystem accents
- warm ivory typography

### Graphic behavior

Orbit lines should feel like **trajectories**, not decorative circles.

Geometric forms should feel **constructed**, reflecting engineering and systems.

Large empty areas should be intentional, representing the concept of open space.

## 11. Applications

The identity should work across:

- Website
- Web applications
- Mobile applications
- Favicon
- App icon
- Git repositories
- Documentation
- Business cards
- Laptop stickers
- Presentations
- Social profiles
- Product splash screens

### Small-size requirement

The standalone symbol should remain recognizable at approximately:

- 512px — full detail
- 192px — normal app/icon use
- 64px — compact digital use
- 32px — favicon/avatar
- 16px — simplified favicon if necessary

At very small sizes, secondary details may be simplified while preserving the core R silhouette and orbital relationship.

## 14. Final Design Intent

RASHA should look like a **technology ecosystem with a human purpose**, not simply another colorful software startup.

The visual identity should communicate:

> **A strong core.  
> A connected ecosystem.  
> Space to grow.  
> A destination worth reaching.**

The design should remain recognizable even when the decorative space elements are removed.
